Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-29

This Privacy Policy applies to the GeoQuiz: Flags & Capitals mobile application (the “Application”) for iOS and Android, published on the App Store and Google Play. By using the Application you agree to this policy. If anything is unclear, please contact us at merbelcorp@outlook.com.

What the Application stores on your device

GeoQuiz is designed to work offline. The following information is stored only on your device in local storage:

  • App preferences: language, theme, sound, haptics, animation and high-contrast toggles.
  • Whether you completed onboarding and whether you granted notification permission.
  • A locally generated random device identifier (a number created on first launch) used to authenticate calls to our backend — see below.
  • Cached copies of data otherwise stored on our servers (your XP, progress, weekly-theme participation) so the game continues to work offline.

What we store on our servers

To power leaderboards, friends, weekly themes and cross-device sign-in, the Application communicates with our backend, hosted on Railway and Supabase (PostgreSQL) inside the European Union. We store the following information against an internal user record that is identified by a random device identifier generated by the Application:

  • Your chosen nickname (up to 21 characters), or an auto-generated placeholder of the form “Traveler #NNNN” if you have not picked one.
  • Your avatar icon, background colour, and (Premium only) decorative frame, picked from a built-in list.
  • A short randomly generated friend code so other players can add you.
  • Your country code (two-letter ISO code reported by your device) used to filter regional leaderboards.
  • Total XP, individual quiz score submissions, and per-mode leaderboard entries (score, duration, attempts).
  • Weekly-theme progress (points earned per day) when you take part in a weekly theme.
  • The friend connections you make and the friend codes you use to add them.
  • An optional encrypted backup of your local game state so progress can be restored when you reinstall or move to a new device.
  • iOS push-notification tokens, only if you grant notification permission (used to deliver streak and weekly-theme reminders).

We do not require an email, phone number, or social-network login to play. The device identifier is a random number generated locally on first launch and is not tied to your real-world identity.

Optional Sign in with Apple

The Application offers an optional “Sign in with Apple” feature on iOS so that your account can be carried between devices (for example, after reinstalling or moving to a new iPhone). If you choose to use it, we additionally store:

  • The stable Apple-issued user identifier (the sub claim) that Apple provides for your account — it is unique to GeoQuiz and is not your Apple ID.
  • The email address Apple shares with us on first sign-in (this may be Apple’s private-relay address that forwards to your real email), used to display “Signed in as …” in Settings.
  • The date the account was linked.

Sign in with Apple is entirely optional — you can play the full game without it. You can disconnect your Apple ID at any time from Settings → Account → Sign out of Apple; this clears the identifiers above from our servers while keeping the rest of your progress. You can also fully delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account, which permanently removes everything described in this section from our servers. We do not sell or share Apple-issued identifiers or email addresses with third parties.

Deleting your data from our servers

You can delete the server-side data described above at any time:

  • Settings → Account → Delete account inside the Application permanently removes your user record and everything attached to it (nickname, avatar, XP, scores, leaderboard entries, friendships, weekly-theme progress, backup, Apple-sign-in binding and push tokens).
  • Or email us at merbelcorp@outlook.com from any address and we will remove the record on request.

Uninstalling the Application removes the locally stored data and prevents further communication with our backend, but does not by itself delete the server-side record — use the in-app option above for that.

Information collected by third-party services

In addition to the GeoQuiz backend described above, the Application uses a small number of third-party providers. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Railway (backend hosting) and Supabase (database hosting) — host the GeoQuiz backend and its PostgreSQL database that store the records listed in “What we store on our servers” above. Both providers may also process basic technical request metadata (IP address, timestamps) for security and abuse prevention. Data is hosted inside the European Union. Railway Privacy, Supabase Privacy.
  • Apple Sign in with Apple (iOS) — optional service used only if you tap “Sign in with Apple” in Settings. Apple issues us a stable user identifier and, on first sign-in, the email address you chose to share (possibly via Apple’s private relay). See the “Optional Sign in with Apple” section above for how we use this data. Sign in with Apple Privacy.
  • Apple Push Notification service (iOS) — used to deliver streak and weekly-theme reminders when you have granted notification permission. We store the APNs device token associated with your user record on our servers; Apple operates the delivery network. Apple Privacy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit (iOS) — handles app delivery, subscription purchases, and basic install/usage analytics that Apple collects on our behalf. Apple Privacy.
  • Google Play / Play Billing (Android) — handles app delivery, subscription purchases, and basic install/usage statistics that Google collects on our behalf. Google Privacy.
  • Google AdMob — serves banner, interstitial and rewarded ads in the free tier. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier and basic technical information to serve and measure ads. Google Privacy.
  • RevenueCat — processes Premium subscription state. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and your purchase receipt; it does not receive your name, email, or profile data. RevenueCat Privacy.
  • Expo / Expo Updates — used to build and, if applicable, deliver over-the-air updates. Expo may receive technical information about your device and app version. Expo Privacy.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — measures aggregate product usage so we can understand which features people use and where to focus improvements. Firebase Analytics processes a Firebase-generated installation identifier and basic technical information (app version, device model, OS version, language, approximate country derived from IP). It does not receive your nickname, avatar, email, exact location, or device advertising identifier. Collection can be turned off at any time from Settings → Analytics inside the Application; see the “Analytics” section below. Firebase Privacy.

Analytics

To improve the Application we collect aggregate, non-personal usage events through Google Firebase Analytics. Examples of events we send: onboarding completed, a practice or Journey session started or finished (with score and accuracy), a level-up or streak milestone reached, the Premium screen opened, a Premium purchase completed, and user feedback submitted. Each event may include simple game parameters (such as session mode, question count, or stage identifier) but never any personal information.

Firebase Analytics also automatically records a small set of standard events on Google’s side, including app open, screen views, and an anonymous session identifier. This data is associated with a Firebase-generated installation ID rather than your name or account.

Your control. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Analytics. When disabled, both our custom events and Firebase’s automatic events stop being sent from your device. This setting is independent from the advertising consent described below — turning off analytics does not affect ads, and turning off personalized ads does not by itself stop analytics.

Advertising and your consent

In the free tier the Application displays banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads can be served in two ways: personalized (targeted using a device advertising identifier and ad-interaction signals) or non-personalized (based only on coarse context such as the app and approximate region). We ask for your consent before serving personalized ads, as described below. Either way, you will see a similar number of ads.

EU, UK and Switzerland (GDPR consent). If your device is located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Application shows a Google-certified consent form on first launch, provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can accept or decline personalized ads there. If you decline, you will still see ads, but non-personalized ones. You can change this choice at any time from Settings → Ad & privacy settings inside the Application.

iOS App Tracking Transparency. On iOS, the Application also presents Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. Personalized ads on iOS are used only if you allow tracking there; if you deny it, ads remain non-personalized regardless of the consent form above. You can revisit this choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Outside the regions listed above, where applicable law does not require a consent form, personalized ads may be served by default; you can still opt out via the iOS prompt or your device’s advertising settings. The fastest way to remove all ads on any platform is to subscribe to Premium.

Subscriptions and purchases

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription managed by the app store you installed from (Apple App Store on iOS, Google Play on Android). We never see or store your payment information. RevenueCat keeps an anonymized record of your entitlement so that the Application can recognize you as a Premium user across reinstalls on the same store account.

Notifications

With your permission, the Application may send local notifications as daily reminders, scheduled on-device. On iOS, it may additionally send remote push notifications (for example streak and weekly-theme reminders) through Apple’s Push Notification service. If you have granted notification permission, our backend stores your APNs device token so it can address these reminders to your device. You can revoke permission at any time from your device’s system Settings for GeoQuiz; revoking permission causes the token to stop working, and it is removed from our servers the next time Apple reports it as invalid.

Children’s privacy

GeoQuiz is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the Application, please contact us at merbelcorp@outlook.com and we will take appropriate action.

Your US privacy rights

If you are a resident of California or another US state with a consumer privacy law (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and others), you have additional rights regarding your personal information.

Categories of information. The categories of personal information involved in this context are: (i) the account data stored on our backend as described in “What we store on our servers” above (nickname, avatar, scores, leaderboard entries, friend connections, weekly-theme progress, optional Apple sign-in identifier and email, device push token, locally generated device identifier); (ii) the device advertising identifier and related technical and ad-interaction data processed by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads in the free tier; and (iii) the Firebase installation identifier and aggregate usage events processed by Google Firebase Analytics for product analytics, which you can turn off as described below.

“Sale” and “sharing.” We do not sell your personal information for money. However, the use of advertising identifiers for personalized ads may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” (for cross-context behavioral advertising) under some US state laws.

Your rights. Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what personal information is processed, to request its deletion, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

How to opt out of personalized ads. You can opt out at any time:

  • In the Application, under Settings → Ad & privacy settings, where applicable a US privacy choice is offered through Google’s consent tools.
  • On iOS, by denying tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, via Settings → Google → Ads.
  • By subscribing to Premium, which removes all ads entirely.

How to delete your server-side account data. You can delete the records described in “What we store on our servers” at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account, or by emailing us at merbelcorp@outlook.com.

You may also exercise any of these rights, or ask a question about them, by emailing us at merbelcorp@outlook.com. We honor a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where it applies.

Your choices

  • Delete server-side account data: in the Application, under Settings → Account → Delete account, or by emailing merbelcorp@outlook.com. Security

Locally stored data is protected by your device’s own protections (passcode, Face ID, full-device encryption); we strongly advise against jailbreaking your device, which can compromise these protections.

Communication between the Application and our backend is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Server-side data is stored in PostgreSQL hosted by Supabase inside the European Union, with access restricted to maintainers of the Application. We do not store passwords (there is no login). The Application authenticates each request with the locally generated random device identifier described above; if you sign in with Apple, your Apple identity token is verified against Apple’s public keys on every sign-in and is never stored in raw form.
