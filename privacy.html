Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-21

This Privacy Policy applies to the GeoQuiz: Flags & Capitals mobile application (the “Application”) for iOS and Android, published on the App Store and Google Play. By using the Application you agree to this policy. If anything is unclear, please contact us at merbelcorp@outlook.com.

What the Application stores on your device

GeoQuiz is designed to work offline. The following information is stored only on your device in local storage and is not transmitted to us:

  • Your in-app profile: chosen nickname (up to 21 characters) and an avatar icon and color picked from a built-in list.
  • Game progress: XP, level, daily streak, per-country mastery, Journey stages and Daily Challenge state.
  • App preferences: language, theme, sound, haptics, animation and high-contrast toggles.
  • Whether you completed onboarding and whether you granted notification permission.

We do not run any backend account system. There is no sign-up, no login, and no cloud sync of your profile or progress. Uninstalling the Application removes all of this data.

Information collected by third-party services

The Application uses a small number of third-party SDKs. Each provider operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit (iOS) — handles app delivery, subscription purchases, and basic install/usage analytics that Apple collects on our behalf. Apple Privacy.
  • Google Play / Play Billing (Android) — handles app delivery, subscription purchases, and basic install/usage statistics that Google collects on our behalf. Google Privacy.
  • Google AdMob — serves banner, interstitial and rewarded ads in the free tier. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier and basic technical information to serve and measure ads. Google Privacy.
  • RevenueCat — processes Premium subscription state. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and your purchase receipt; it does not receive your name, email, or profile data. RevenueCat Privacy.
  • Expo / Expo Updates — used to build and, if applicable, deliver over-the-air updates. Expo may receive technical information about your device and app version. Expo Privacy.

Advertising and your consent

In the free tier the Application displays banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads can be served in two ways: personalized (targeted using a device advertising identifier and ad-interaction signals) or non-personalized (based only on coarse context such as the app and approximate region). We ask for your consent before serving personalized ads, as described below. Either way, you will see a similar number of ads.

EU, UK and Switzerland (GDPR consent). If your device is located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Application shows a Google-certified consent form on first launch, provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can accept or decline personalized ads there. If you decline, you will still see ads, but non-personalized ones. You can change this choice at any time from Settings → Ad & privacy settings inside the Application.

iOS App Tracking Transparency. On iOS, the Application also presents Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. Personalized ads on iOS are used only if you allow tracking there; if you deny it, ads remain non-personalized regardless of the consent form above. You can revisit this choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Outside the regions listed above, where applicable law does not require a consent form, personalized ads may be served by default; you can still opt out via the iOS prompt or your device’s advertising settings. The fastest way to remove all ads on any platform is to subscribe to Premium.

Subscriptions and purchases

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription managed by the app store you installed from (Apple App Store on iOS, Google Play on Android). We never see or store your payment information. RevenueCat keeps an anonymized record of your entitlement so that the Application can recognize you as a Premium user across reinstalls on the same store account.

Notifications

With your permission, the Application may send local notifications as daily reminders. Notifications are scheduled on-device. We do not run a push-notification server and do not have a list of your device tokens.

Children’s privacy

GeoQuiz is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the Application, please contact us at merbelcorp@outlook.com and we will take appropriate action.

Your US privacy rights

If you are a resident of California or another US state with a consumer privacy law (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and others), you have additional rights regarding your personal information.

Categories of information. As described above, GeoQuiz itself does not collect personal information on a backend; game data stays on your device. The only category of information involved in this context is the device advertising identifier and related technical and ad-interaction data processed by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads in the free tier.

“Sale” and “sharing.” We do not sell your personal information for money. However, the use of advertising identifiers for personalized ads may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” (for cross-context behavioral advertising) under some US state laws.

Your rights. Depending on your state, you may have the right to know what personal information is processed, to request its deletion, to correct it, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

How to opt out of personalized ads. You can opt out at any time:

  • In the Application, under Settings → Ad & privacy settings, where applicable a US privacy choice is offered through Google’s consent tools.
  • On iOS, by denying tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, via Settings → Google → Ads.
  • By subscribing to Premium, which removes all ads entirely.

You may also exercise any of these rights, or ask a question about them, by emailing us at merbelcorp@outlook.com. We honor a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where it applies.

Your choices

  • Delete all data: uninstall the Application from your device.
  • Stop ads: subscribe to Premium, which removes all ads.
  • Personalized-ads consent: in the Application, under Settings → Ad & privacy settings.
  • Manage Premium: iOS — Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; Android — Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Ad tracking (iOS): Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad personalization (Android): Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Notifications: in your device’s system Settings for GeoQuiz.

Security

Because game data lives on your device, its security is governed by your device’s own protections (passcode, Face ID, full-device encryption). We strongly advise against jailbreaking your device, which can compromise these protections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL with an updated effective date. Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store listing.

Contact

Questions or data requests? Email merbelcorp@outlook.com and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
