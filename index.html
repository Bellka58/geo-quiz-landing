Features

Built to make geography stick.

Four ways to play, all designed around short, focused sessions you can fit between stops on the metro.

Daily Challenge & Weekly Theme

A fixed set of questions every day plus a 7-day themed run — build streaks, climb the weekly theme, keep the habit small enough to stick.

Practice your way

Flags, Capitals, or Custom rounds — pick regions, round size and progress filter. Spaced repetition quietly surfaces the countries you keep missing.

Journey by region

Work through stages grouped by continent and difficulty — Europe Easy, Asia Easy, all the way to Master tier — at your own pace.

Achievements & XP

Earn XP for every correct answer, level up through a 500-level curve, and unlock achievements as your accuracy and streak grow.
